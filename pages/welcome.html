Save it now.
Hear it later.

Add articles as you find them. QueueTTS reads them aloud one after another, and picks up from the sentence where you stopped, even after you restart Chrome.

Choose how it sounds

QueueTTS uses the voices Chrome provides. You can change this later in Settings.

  1. Pin it

    Open the puzzle-piece menu in Chrome’s toolbar and pin QueueTTS, so it’s one click away.

  2. Add what you want to hear

    Use the toolbar button on any article, or right-click the page. Select text first to add only that part.

  3. Listen from anywhere

    Play from the toolbar button, or open the side panel to see your queue and read along.

Your queue and everything you add are stored in this browser profile. QueueTTS has no account, no server and no analytics.
