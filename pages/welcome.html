QueueTTS

Listen to the web later, in order.

Add articles as you find them. QueueTTS reads them aloud one after another, and remembers the sentence you stopped on.

  1. Pin it

    Open the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar and pin QueueTTS, so its button is always one click away.

  2. Add pages

    Use the toolbar button, right-click a page, or select text first to add just that part.

  3. Listen anywhere

    Play from the toolbar button, or open the side panel to see the whole queue and follow along.

Everything you add stays in this browser. There’s no account and no server.
